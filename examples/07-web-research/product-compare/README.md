# Compare products before buying

**Tool:** Web research · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
You need new equipment and want an honest comparison of two models.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Compare these two printers for a small office: price, running cost and reliability.
```

![A side-by-side product comparison](img/product-compare.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent researches both products and lays out a clear comparison so you can pick the better fit for your budget and use.

## Good to know
Ask for the total cost of ownership, not just the sticker price.
