# The assistant in your team group

**Tool:** Telegram · **How you reach it:** Telegram group

## The situation
Your team has a group chat. Adding the agent means everyone can ask it things.

## What you ask the agent
```text
(telegram group) What are today's delivery stops?
```

![The agent answering in a team group chat](img/team-group.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent joins the group and answers the team's questions with the shared information, so nobody has to be the walking encyclopedia.

## Good to know
You control who is allowed in the group and what the agent may share.
