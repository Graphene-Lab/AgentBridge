# Draft a service contract

**Tool:** Document tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
A new client wants everything in writing before starting. You need a simple contract that covers the work, the price and the dates.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Draft a service contract between my studio and a client for a 3-month website project at 6,000 euros, with a 50% deposit.
```

![A drafted service agreement, ready for review](img/service-contract.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent produces a clear contract with the parties, the scope of work, the payment schedule, and the timeline. It is a starting point you can review and adjust — not legal advice, but a solid draft that saves you hours of blank-page work.

## Good to know
Attach your old contract and ask it to follow the same style and clauses.
