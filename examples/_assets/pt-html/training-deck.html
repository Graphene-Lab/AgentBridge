Our product in one page

What is Nimbus?

A project-management SaaS that helps teams plan projects, assign tasks, track progress and share documents in one workspace.

Who uses it?

8,000 teams — from startups to large enterprises — rely on Nimbus every day to keep their work organized.

What Nimbus does, in four pillars

Plan projects

Timelines, milestones and roadmaps in a single view.

Assign tasks

Owners, due dates and priorities for every piece of work.

Track progress

The ticket workflow

1. Ticket arrives

A new request lands in the helpdesk console.

→

2. Acknowledge

Reply within 15 minutes.

→

3. Diagnose

Find the root cause of the issue.

→

4. Solve or escalate

Fix it, or hand it off cleanly.

→

5. Confirm

Verify the fix with the customer.

→

6. Close

Send the satisfaction survey.

SLA at a glance: acknowledge in 15 min · resolve or escalate in 30 min · close with a survey.

How to escalate

Escalate when…

The ticket is unresolved after 30 minutes.

The customer is upset or the situation is sensitive.

Escalation checklist

1. Write a clear summary

What happened, what you tried, what you need.

2. Tag a senior agent

Assign the ticket to the right owner.

3. Keep the customer informed

Tell them it is being handled.

4. Log every step

The next agent needs full context.

Best practices

Search the knowledge base first

Most answers already exist. Check before you write.

Never promise a date you cannot keep

Under-promise and over-deliver — trust is everything.

Log every step

A well-documented ticket saves the whole team time.

Aim for a CSAT of 4.5 or higher

Every interaction is a chance to earn a great score.

Summary & key takeaways

The essentials

  • • Know the product: four pillars, 8,000 teams.
  • • Speak plainly: friendly, clear, concise, no jargon.
  • • Follow the flow: acknowledge in 15 min, resolve in 30.
  • • Escalate early with a clear summary and full logs.
  • • Use the knowledge base, status page and engineering chat.
  • • Document everything and protect the customer's trust.
