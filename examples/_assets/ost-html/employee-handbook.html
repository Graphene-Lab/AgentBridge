1. Purpose and Audience

This handbook provides all employees of Acme Studio with clear guidance on working hours, holidays, remote work, dress code, and who to contact for help. It is designed to make your experience here smooth, fair, and transparent.

This handbook applies to all employees of Acme Studio, including full-time and part-time staff. Please read it carefully and reach out with any questions. Our values are simple: be kind, be clear, and ask early rather than struggle alone.

3. Getting Started

Welcome to Acme Studio! Here is what you need to know to settle in quickly and comfortably:

  1. Introduce yourself to your team lead, Anna, who will guide you through your day-to-day work and answer any questions about your role.
  2. Meet Maria in HR to review your contract, pay details, and set up your time-off tracking.
  3. Collect your equipment from Luca in IT, including your laptop, peripherals, and any software access you need.
  4. Familiarise yourself with the core hours (10:00 to 16:00) and make sure you are reachable during that window, whether you are in the office or working remotely.
4. Main Procedures

4.1 Working Hours and Core Hours

  1. Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00, with a one-hour lunch break.
  2. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. During this time, all employees should be reachable and available for meetings and collaboration.
  3. If you need to adjust your schedule outside core hours, discuss it with your team lead, Anna, in advance.

4.2 Holidays and Time Off

  1. Every employee receives 26 paid vacation days per year, in addition to national holidays.
  2. Submit your time-off request at least two weeks in advance through your team lead, Anna.
  3. For questions about your remaining balance, holiday pay, or special leave, contact Maria in HR.

4.3 Remote Work

  1. You may work from home up to two days per week. Coordinate your remote days with your team lead to ensure coverage.
  2. During remote work, you must have a stable internet connection and be available during core hours (10:00 to 16:00).
  3. All necessary equipment (laptop, peripherals, software) is provided by the company. Contact Luca in IT if you need additional hardware or support.

4.4 Dress Code

  1. In the office, the dress code is smart casual. Aim for neat, comfortable attire that is appropriate for a professional environment.
  2. When working from home and joining video calls, dress comfortably but presentably.
  3. There is no strict uniform policy; use your judgement and when in doubt, ask your team lead.
5. Troubleshooting
Issue Probable Cause Solution
Cannot reach team lead for a work question Team lead is in a meeting or away from desk Send a message and wait for a reply. If urgent, contact Maria in HR as a backup. Remember: ask early rather than struggle alone.
Remote work connection is unstable Home internet issues or overloaded network Restart your router, close bandwidth-heavy applications, or use a mobile hotspot. If the problem persists, notify your team lead and consider coming into the office.
Time-off request was denied Insufficient notice or team coverage conflict Discuss the reason with your team lead, Anna. If you believe the decision is unfair, escalate to Maria in HR for review.
Equipment issue while working remotely Hardware malfunction or software error Contact Luca in IT immediately. Do not attempt repairs yourself. The company will arrange a replacement or remote fix.
6. Safety and Maintenance
  • Keep your workspace tidy and free of trip hazards. In the office, report any safety concerns to your team lead immediately.
  • Take care of company-provided equipment. Report any damage or malfunction to Luca in IT as soon as possible.
  • Protect company data at all times. Use strong passwords, lock your screen when stepping away, and never share credentials.
  • When working from home, set up an ergonomic workspace to avoid strain. Take regular breaks and stretch during long sessions.
