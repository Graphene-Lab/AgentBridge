1. Executive Summary

Acme Studio closed fiscal year 2025 with revenue of EUR 152,000, total costs of EUR 88,000 and a net profit of EUR 64,000. Compared with fiscal year 2024, revenue increased from EUR 118,000 while costs rose only modestly from EUR 77,000, resulting in a significantly improved profitability position.

Revenue grew 29% year over year while costs stayed controlled, lifting the net margin. Net profit rose from EUR 41,000 in 2024 to EUR 64,000 in 2025, an increase of approximately 56%. The year-over-year comparison confirms that growth was achieved without a proportional increase in the cost base.

3. Revenue Analysis

Revenue for the Fiscal Year 2025 reached EUR 152,000, an increase of 29% compared with the previous period.

Revenue rose from EUR 118,000 in fiscal year 2024 to EUR 152,000 in fiscal year 2025, an increase of EUR 34,000 or 29% year over year. This growth reflects continued demand for Acme Studio's services throughout the period and represents the strongest top-line result recorded for the comparison presented in this report.

4. Cost Analysis

Cost of goods sold amounted to EUR 88,000, while operating expenses are included within this total, yielding a gross margin of 42.1%.

Total costs for fiscal year 2025 were EUR 88,000, compared with EUR 77,000 in fiscal year 2024, an increase of EUR 11,000 or approximately 14%. Costs therefore grew at roughly half the rate of revenue, remaining controlled and confirming that the revenue expansion was not accompanied by a proportional increase in the cost base.

6. Outlook

On the basis of the fiscal year 2025 results, Acme Studio enters the next period with a strengthened profit position. Revenue grew 29% year over year while costs stayed controlled, lifting the net margin. Management intends to preserve this balance between top-line growth and cost discipline in order to sustain the improved net margin achieved in 2025.

7. Risks
  • Revenue concentration: the 29% year-over-year revenue growth may depend on a limited number of clients or engagements.
  • Cost pressure: total costs increased by 14% year over year and could rise further if not kept under control.
  • Margin sustainability: the improved net margin of 42.1% must be maintained as revenue scales.
  • Reporting scope: liquidity and solvency data are not available for fiscal year 2025, limiting the completeness of the financial assessment.
