1. Executive Summary

Acme Studio is pleased to submit this proposal to Bright Cafe for the creation of a simple online store that allows customers to order ahead. The project covers the design, build and launch of a branded online shop with a product catalogue, secure checkout and order email notifications. The total investment is EUR 9,500, delivered over an 8-week timeline.

2. Understanding of Your Needs

Bright Cafe needs a straightforward digital ordering channel that complements its in-store operations and makes it easy for customers to order ahead.

  • Customers need a simple way to browse the menu and place orders before arriving at the cafe.
  • The online shop must reflect the Bright Cafe brand and feel consistent with the in-store experience.
  • Ordering must be secure and reliable, with clear confirmation sent to both the customer and the cafe.
  • The solution should be easy to manage and ready to go live quickly, without unnecessary complexity.
3. Proposed Solution

Acme Studio proposes a focused online shop built around order-ahead functionality, delivered in three clear phases: Design, Build and Launch.

  • A branded online shop designed to match the Bright Cafe identity and work smoothly on mobile and desktop.
  • A structured product catalogue that makes it easy for customers to find and select items.
  • A secure checkout process that protects customer payment and personal data.
  • Automated order email notifications so the cafe and its customers receive immediate confirmation.
