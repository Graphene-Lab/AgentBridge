# Never miss a deadline

**Tool:** Scheduled tasks · **How you reach it:** Agreed schedule

## The situation
A bill is due every month and you keep almost forgetting it.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Remind me on the 25th of every month that the supplier invoice is due.
```

![A recurring reminder set for a monthly deadline](img/recurring-reminder.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent reminds you on the day, every month. You set it once and stop worrying about the date.

## On a schedule
A monthly recurring reminder, delivered however you like — chat, email or phone.
