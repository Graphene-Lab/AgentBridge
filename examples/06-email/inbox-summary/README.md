# Catch up on your inbox

**Tool:** Email tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
You were away and the inbox piled up. You want the short version: what needs you now.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Summarise my unread emails and tell me which ones need a reply today.
```

![A short summary of the inbox with the urgent items](img/inbox-summary.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent reads your unread mail and gives you a short list: what is urgent, what can wait, and what you can ignore. You tackle the real priorities first.

## Good to know
A morning summary can be scheduled so this is waiting for you with your coffee.
