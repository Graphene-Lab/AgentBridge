# A polite follow-up

**Tool:** Email tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
A client has not answered your quote. A short, friendly follow-up keeps it moving without being pushy.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Write a short follow-up to a client who has not replied to our quote from last week.
```

![A friendly follow-up email draft](img/follow-up.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent writes a light, polite nudge that reminds without pressure. You send it and keep the relationship warm.

## Good to know
A scheduled follow-up can send these for you if a reply has not arrived.
