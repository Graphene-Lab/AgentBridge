# Answer an email in your voice

**Tool:** Email tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
A customer email needs a warm, clear reply. You tell the agent the gist; it writes the message.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Reply to this customer thanking them and confirming we will ship tomorrow: [paste email]
```

![A drafted email reply, ready to send](img/quick-reply.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent writes a friendly, professional reply that sounds like you. You read it once, press send, and move on.

## Good to know
Set up your mail once with /email. After that, reading and sending work from the chat.
