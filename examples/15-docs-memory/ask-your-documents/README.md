# Ask questions about your own files

**Tool:** Documents area + File tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
Your answers are inside your own files. You ask a question and the agent finds it in your documents.

## What you ask the agent
```text
What is our cancellation policy with the printing supplier?
```

![The agent answering from your own documents](img/ask-your-documents.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent searches your documents area and answers with what your own files actually say, pointing to the source.

## Good to know
Keep your business files in the documents area and they become searchable knowledge.
