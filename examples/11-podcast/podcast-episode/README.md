# Turn a topic into a podcast

**Tool:** Podcast tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
You want a short audio piece for your audience — an interview-style episode on a topic you know.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Create a 5-minute podcast episode about why small shops should go online, in a friendly two-voice style.
```

![A podcast episode ready to play](img/podcast-episode.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent writes the script and produces an audio episode with two voices, ready to publish. Your message, in audio form, with no studio.

## Good to know
Give it your key points and it shapes them into a natural conversation.
