# Find a customer fast

**Tool:** ERP tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
On the phone, you need a customer's details quickly.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Find the customer Maria Rossi and show her contact and last order.
```

![A customer record retrieved on request](img/customer-lookup.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent finds the customer and shows the contact details and the last order, so you can help without putting them on hold.

## Good to know
Ask for the last three orders if you want the full picture.
