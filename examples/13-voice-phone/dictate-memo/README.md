# Speak instead of type

**Tool:** Voice (dictation) · **How you reach it:** Voice input

## The situation
You have an idea while your hands are busy. You speak it and the agent writes it down.

## What you ask the agent
```text
(speak) Take a note: call the supplier about the delayed shipment tomorrow morning.
```

![A spoken note captured by the agent](img/dictate-memo.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent listens and writes the note for you. Your hands stay free; the thought is saved.

## Good to know
Dictation works for messages, notes and even whole emails — just talk.
