# Track your sales

**Tool:** Spreadsheet tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
You want one sheet that shows sales by month and where they came from, so you can spot what is working.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Create a sales tracker with month, channel and revenue, and a chart of revenue by channel.
```

![A sales tracker with revenue by channel](img/sales-tracker.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent builds the tracker and the chart. Add rows as you go, or attach your raw sales list and ask it to fill the sheet for you.

## Good to know
Attach a messy export from your shop and say 'clean this into a tracker' — it will.
