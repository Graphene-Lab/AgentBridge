# A budget you can read

**Tool:** Spreadsheet tools · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
You want to see planned versus actual spending for the month, with a chart, without fighting a spreadsheet for an hour.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Build a monthly budget spreadsheet with planned and actual for rent, marketing and salaries, plus a chart.
```

![A budget spreadsheet with a clear bar chart](img/monthly-budget.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent creates the spreadsheet with the categories, the planned and actual columns, totals, and a chart that shows the difference at a glance. You can open it in Excel and keep working.

## Good to know
Ask for the chart title and the currency so it matches your business.
