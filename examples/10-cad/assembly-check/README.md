# Check how parts fit

**Tool:** CAD tools (FreeCAD) · **How you reach it:** Chat message

## The situation
You want to see if two parts fit together before making them.

## What you ask the agent
```text
Place these two parts together and check for any overlap or clearance issues.
```

![An assembly check between two parts](img/assembly-check.png)

## What AgentBridge does
The agent assembles the parts in the CAD model and points out where they clash or where the fit is too tight.

## Good to know
Catch fit problems on screen, not on the workshop bench.
