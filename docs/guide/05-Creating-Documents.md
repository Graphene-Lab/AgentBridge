# Creating documents from a simple request

One of the most impressive things about your assistant is that it produces finished office documents from a plain request. You describe what you need, and the agent designs and builds it for you: an invoice, an employment contract, a spreadsheet with data and charts, a presentation with slides, or a detailed PDF report. No templates to fill in and no formatting work — the document arrives ready to use.

## Documents, spreadsheets, and presentations

Ask for a Word document and the agent writes the text and lays it out for you. Ask for a spreadsheet and it prepares the data, applies the styles, adds a chart, and sets up the page for printing. Ask for a presentation and it designs the slides, choosing the layout and the content for each one. You can attach your own material, so the document reflects your real data and not generic examples.

## Detailed PDF reports

For market analysis, financial reports, or any analytical document, the assistant produces accurate PDF reports with professional sources and a level of detail that is hard to match by hand. Just describe the topic and the depth you need, and the report comes back as a finished PDF.

## A personal style

The assistant remembers the style it used for your documents and keeps it for future work. If you want a different look, you simply ask, and it adjusts the result to your taste.
