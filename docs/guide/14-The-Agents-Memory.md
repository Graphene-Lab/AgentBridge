# The assistant's memory

Your assistant remembers. Every conversation leaves a trace, and every new request instantly brings back the facts, the people, and the documents it has already handled. Ask it something it worked on last week, and it answers as if it had never forgotten — because it has not.

## A precise memory, not a vague one

Many assistants try to remember by re-reading everything and guessing what is relevant. AgentBridge works differently: its memory is deterministic, which means it retrieves exactly the relevant context, with no wasted effort. The result is an assistant that answers faster and never confuses your project with someone else's, even after months of work.

## What it remembers

The assistant remembers the style you prefer for your documents, the facts you have told it, and everything it has produced for you. This is why it can keep a consistent look across your reports and presentations, and why you do not have to repeat yourself from one conversation to the next. The memory lives on your machine, in your own folders, like everything else.

## The memory in your hands

You are always in control. The memory is tied to your documents area and your conversations, and it works for you: the more you use the assistant, the better it knows your work, and the less you have to explain.
