# Web research: facts and sources from the internet

When your work needs up-to-date information, the assistant can browse the web for you. You ask a question, and it searches, visits the relevant pages, gathers the facts, and gives you an answer with its sources. You can ask for a quick summary of a topic, a comparison of products, or a full research report with references you can verify.

## Research agents

AgentBridge includes agents specialised for this kind of work. The web agent combines browsing with your files, so it can blend what it finds online with your own documents. The research and search agents focus on gathering and summarising information. You can switch to them by typing /tools and choosing the one that fits the task, without ever leaving the chat.

## Reports you can trust

Because the assistant keeps track of where each fact comes from, you can check the sources yourself and see how it reached its conclusions. This makes it a reliable helper for market analysis, competitor reviews, product research, and any report that must be grounded in real evidence rather than guesses.

## Privacy while browsing

Web research is the one task where information leaves your machine, because the assistant must reach the internet to find it. Your documents are never sent along with the request, so the browsing stays private while the rest of your work remains local.
